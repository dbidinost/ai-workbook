How to use this
  • One canvas per session, one screen each. You fill it in live, in the room.
  • Nothing is asked twice. Where a later canvas needs an earlier answer, it points you back rather than asking you to retype it.
  • The prompts (P1–P6) live in the Reference Guide. You do not need to open it before the session.
  • Italic text is guidance. It names the mistake most people make in that box.
  • Save keeps your work against your email so you can pick it up later on any device. Export downloads a copy to this device. Submit sends it to the facilitators.

Your email is how your workbook is saved and picked up again. It is required to Save or Submit.
